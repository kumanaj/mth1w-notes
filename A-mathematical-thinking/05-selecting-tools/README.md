# Selecting Tools and Computational Strategies

> 📘 **Curriculum:** A1 · 📖 **Textbook:** §1.5, pp. 23–28 · [⬅ Strand A: Mathematical Thinking and Making Connections](../)

## Big idea
Choose the **right tool** for the job: mental math, pencil and paper, calculator, grid paper,
physical models (tiles, cubes), or a computer. Sometimes you need more than one.

## Vocabulary
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **pentomino** | a shape made of five unit squares, each sharing at least one full side with another |

## Key concepts
- The best tool is the one that is **fastest and accurate enough**:
  - $10 + 20$ → mental math, not a calculator
  - length of a soccer field → trundle wheel, not a 30-cm ruler
- Computing with integers and fractions is covered in [Rational Numbers (Strand B)](../../B-number/04-rational-numbers/).
- **Don't round in the middle** of a calculation. Use fraction keys or keep full decimals.

## Worked examples

### Example 1: Ted's calculator error
Ted evaluated $\frac{5}{9} + \frac{2}{3} - \frac{1}{8}$ as $0.6 + 0.7 - 0.1 = 1.2$.
He **rounded too early**. Exact:
$\frac{40}{72} + \frac{48}{72} - \frac{9}{72} = \frac{79}{72} \approx 1.097$.
Use the fraction key, or keep all digits until the end.

### Example 2: Sums of cubes (pattern tool)
$1^3 = 1,\; 1^3 + 2^3 = 9,\; 1^3 + 2^3 + 3^3 = 36,\; \ldots$ These are perfect squares:
$1^2, 3^2, 6^2, \ldots$, i.e. $(1 + 2 + \cdots + n)^2$.
So the first 15 cubes sum to $(1 + 2 + \cdots + 15)^2 = 120^2 = 14\,400$.

## Watch out
- ⚠️ Division by zero is **undefined**: $12 \div 0$ has no answer.
- ⚠️ A calculator only helps if you key in the order of operations correctly. Use brackets.

## Quick check
1. Which tool would you use, and why? (a) $25 \times 4$ (b) the length of a hallway (c) $\sqrt{90\,224\,199}$ to 2 decimal places
2. Find the 1000th term of $45, 41, 37, 33, \ldots$

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. (a) Mental math, since it's 100. (b) A measuring tape or trundle wheel. (c) A calculator.
2. Term $n = 45 - 4(n - 1)$, so term 1000 $= 45 - 4(999) = -3951$.

</details>
