# Strand E: Geometry and Measurement

> [⬅ Course home](../) · 📘 [Ontario MTH1W curriculum](https://www.dcp.edu.gov.on.ca/en/curriculum/secondary-mathematics/courses/mth1w)

Geometric relationships (angles, circles, triangles), the Pythagorean theorem, surface area and volume, how changing dimensions affects measurements, and measurement systems. In the textbook this is **Chapters 7, 8 and 9**.

## Overall expectations

- **E1 Geometric and Measurement Relationships**: demonstrate an understanding of the development and use of geometric and measurement relationships, and apply these relationships to solve problems, including problems involving real-life situations

## Formula sheet
| Shape | Surface area | Volume |
|-------|--------------|--------|
| Rectangular prism | $2lw + 2lh + 2wh$ | $lwh$ |
| Cylinder | $2\pi r^2 + 2\pi r h$ | $\pi r^2 h$ |
| Pyramid | base + triangular faces | $\frac{1}{3}(\text{base area})h$ |
| Cone | $\pi r s + \pi r^2$ | $\frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$ |
| Sphere | $4\pi r^2$ | $\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$ |
