## Curriculum expectations

- **E1.1**: research a geometric concept or a measurement system to tell a story about its development and use in a specific culture or community, and describe its relevance in connection to careers and to other disciplines
- **E1.3**: solve problems involving different units within a measurement system and between measurement systems, including those from various cultures or communities, using various representations and technology, when appropriate

## Textbook sections

- Partly: metric prefixes and powers of 10 in §3.3 (p. 119)

## Key ideas to learn

- [ ] Convert within the metric system (km, m, cm, mm; L, mL; kg, g) using powers of 10
- [ ] Convert between metric and imperial: 1 in = 2.54 cm, 1 ft = 12 in, 1 mi ≈ 1.61 km, 1 lb ≈ 0.454 kg
- [ ] Area and volume units scale by the square and the cube: $1 \text{ m}^2 = 10\,000 \text{ cm}^2$, $1 \text{ m}^3 = 1\,000\,000 \text{ cm}^3$
- [ ] E1.1 research: a measurement system from a specific culture or community and its use in careers
