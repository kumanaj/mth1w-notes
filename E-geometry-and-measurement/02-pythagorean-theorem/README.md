## Key ideas to learn

- [ ] $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$, where $c$ is the hypotenuse
- [ ] Find a missing side: $c = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$ or $a = \sqrt{c^2 - b^2}$
- [ ] Perimeter and area of **composite shapes**: split into rectangles, triangles and circles, then add or subtract
