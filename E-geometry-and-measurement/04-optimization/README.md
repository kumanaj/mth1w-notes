## Key ideas to learn

- [ ] For a fixed perimeter, a **square** gives the largest rectangle area
- [ ] For a fixed area, a square gives the smallest perimeter
- [ ] For a fixed volume, a **cube** gives the least surface area of square-based prisms
- [ ] For a cylinder, the minimum surface area for a given volume occurs when the height equals the diameter
