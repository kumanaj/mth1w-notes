## Curriculum expectations

- **E1.4**: show how changing one or more dimensions of a two-dimensional shape and a three-dimensional object affects perimeter/circumference, area, surface area, and volume, using technology when appropriate
- **E1.6**: solve problems using the relationships between the volume of prisms and pyramids and between the volume of cylinders and cones, involving various units of measure

## Key ideas to learn

- [ ] Prism: $V = (\text{area of base}) \times h$; pyramid: $V = \frac{1}{3}(\text{area of base}) \times h$
- [ ] Cylinder $V = \pi r^2 h$; cone $V = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$ (E1.6: a cone is $\frac{1}{3}$ of a cylinder with the same base and height)
- [ ] Cone: $SA = \pi r s + \pi r^2$ ($s$ = slant height); sphere: $SA = 4\pi r^2$, $V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$
- [ ] E1.4: how changing one dimension changes the perimeter, area, surface area and volume (doubling every length makes the area 4× and the volume 8×)
