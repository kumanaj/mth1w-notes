## Key ideas to learn

- [ ] Interior angles of a triangle sum to 180°; an exterior angle equals the sum of the two opposite interior angles
- [ ] Interior angles of a quadrilateral sum to 360°; of an $n$-gon, $180°(n - 2)$
- [ ] Exterior angles of any convex polygon sum to 360°
- [ ] Circle properties (E1.2), e.g. an angle inscribed in a semicircle is 90°
- [ ] Create and analyse designs that use these relationships (e.g. with dynamic geometry software)
