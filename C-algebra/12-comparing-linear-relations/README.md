## Curriculum expectations

- **C3.3**: compare two linear relations of the form y = ax + b graphically and algebraically, and interpret the meaning of their point of intersection in terms of a given context

## Textbook sections

- 6.7 Linear Systems (p. 344)
- Chapter 6 Review (p. 352), Practice Test (p. 354), Chapters 4 to 6 Review (p. 356), Tasks (p. 358)

## Key ideas to learn

- [ ] Graph two relations $y = ax + b$ on the same grid and find the **point of intersection**
- [ ] Find the intersection algebraically by setting the two expressions equal
- [ ] Interpret the point in context, e.g. the break-even point for two phone plans: before it one plan is cheaper, after it the other is
