# Lesson 5: Modelling With Algebra (Word Problems)

> 📖 Textbook §4.5, pp. 220–229 · [⬅ Solving Equations](README.md)

## Big idea
Turn the words into an equation, solve it, then turn the answer back into words.

## The 5-step plan
1. **Let** a variable stand for the unknown. Usually it's the *smallest* or *simplest* quantity: "Let $k$ = Koko's pay."
2. **Write expressions** for everything else in terms of that variable. A table helps.
3. **Write an equation** using the fact the problem gives you (a total, a perimeter, an equal cost…).
4. **Solve** it.
5. **Answer the question** in a sentence with units, and **check** it against the original words.

## Translating words into algebra
| Words | Algebra (number = $n$) |
|-------|------------------------|
| four more than a number; a number increased by 4 | $n + 4$ |
| five less than a number | $n - 5$ ⚠️ (not $5 - n$) |
| five less than double a number | $2n - 5$ |
| triple a number | $3n$ |
| half a number | $\frac{n}{2}$ |
| the sum of a number and 6 is 20 | $n + 6 = 20$ |
| 3 consecutive integers | $n,\; n + 1,\; n + 2$ |
| 3 consecutive **even** (or odd) integers | $n,\; n + 2,\; n + 4$ |
| Rufio is 5 years older than Hanna | Hanna $= h$, Rufio $= h + 5$ |
| "is", "equals", "gives", "results in" | $=$ |

## Worked examples

### Example 1: Payroll (organize with a table)
*Jerry earns twice as much as Koko. Their dad earns 200 dollars a week more than Jerry. The total weekly payroll is 1450 dollars. How much does each person earn?*

| Person | Expression |
|--------|------------|
| Koko | $k$ |
| Jerry | $2k$ |
| Dad | $2k + 200$ |

$$
\begin{aligned}
k + 2k + (2k + 200) &= 1450 \\
5k + 200 &= 1450 \\
5k &= 1250 \\
k &= 250
\end{aligned}
$$
**Koko earns 250 dollars, Jerry earns 500 dollars, and their dad earns 700 dollars per week.**
Check: $250 + 500 + 700 = 1450$ ✓

### Example 2: Wage plus commission (a formula)
*Uma earns 6 dollars/h plus 50¢ for every bag of peanuts she sells.*

Earnings formula: $E = 6h + 0.5p$ ($h$ = hours, $p$ = bags)

a) 4 hours and 42 bags: $E = 6(4) + 0.5(42) = 24 + 21 = 45$, so **she earns 45 dollars**.

b) How many bags must she sell to earn 100 dollars in 7 hours?
$$
\begin{aligned}
100 &= 6(7) + 0.5p \\
100 &= 42 + 0.5p \\
58 &= 0.5p \\
p &= 116
\end{aligned}
$$
**She must sell 116 bags.** (Dividing by 0.5 is the same as multiplying by 2.)

### Example 3: Ages
*Rufio is 5 years older than his sister Hanna. The sum of their ages is 37.*
$$h + (h + 5) = 37 \;\Rightarrow\; 2h + 5 = 37 \;\Rightarrow\; 2h = 32 \;\Rightarrow\; h = 16$$
**Hanna is 16 and Rufio is 21.**

### Example 4: Comparing two options
*You have 2000 dollars to rent a hall. Royal James Hall charges 50 dollars per person. Broadway Nights charges 1000 dollars plus 30 dollars per person. Which hall lets you invite more people?*

| Royal James | Broadway Nights |
|---|---|
| $50n = 2000$ | $1000 + 30n = 2000$ |
| $n = 40$ | $30n = 1000$ |
| | $n = 33.\overline{3} \to 33$ (round down) |

**Royal James Hall lets you invite 40 people, compared with 33.**

## Watch out
- ⚠️ Define your variable in words ("Let $k$ = Koko's weekly pay in dollars").
- ⚠️ Solving for $k$ isn't the end. Answer **every part** of the question.
- ⚠️ "Less than" switches the order: "7 less than $x$" is $x - 7$.
- ⚠️ Make sure the answer makes sense (no negative lengths, no fractions of people).

## Quick check
1. Write as algebra: (a) triple a number (b) four more than a number (c) five less than double a number
2. Three consecutive integers add to 72. Find them.
3. A rink supplier charges 40 dollars per jersey plus a 75-dollar logo fee. How many jerseys can a team buy with 700 dollars?
4. A rectangle's length is 3 cm more than twice its width. Its perimeter is 54 cm. Find its dimensions.

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. (a) $3n$ (b) $n + 4$ (c) $2n - 5$
2. $n + (n + 1) + (n + 2) = 72 \Rightarrow 3n + 3 = 72 \Rightarrow n = 23$. **23, 24 and 25.**
3. $40n + 75 = 700 \Rightarrow 40n = 625 \Rightarrow n = 15.6$. Round down: **15 jerseys**.
4. Width $= w$, length $= 2w + 3$. Then $2w + 2(2w + 3) = 54 \Rightarrow 6w + 6 = 54 \Rightarrow w = 8$. **8 cm by 19 cm.** Check: $2(8) + 2(19) = 54$ ✓

</details>
