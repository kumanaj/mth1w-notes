# Lesson 1: Simple Equations

> 📖 Textbook §4.1, pp. 186–195 · [⬅ Solving Equations](README.md)

## Big idea
Get the variable **alone** by doing the **opposite operation** to both sides.

## Three ways to solve
1. **Inspection**: just think. "A number plus 4 is 13 → the number is 9." Good for easy numbers.
2. **Balance method**: picture algebra tiles on a scale and remove the same tiles from both sides.
3. **Opposite operations**: the algebra way. Works for every equation, so **use this on quizzes**.

## One-step equations

### Example 1: Adding and subtracting
$$
\begin{aligned}
x + 4 &= 13 \\
x + 4 - 4 &= 13 - 4 && \text{undo } +4\\
x &= 9
\end{aligned}
\qquad\qquad
\begin{aligned}
x - 8 &= 2 \\
x - 8 + 8 &= 2 + 8 && \text{undo } -8\\
x &= 10
\end{aligned}
$$

The variable can be on the right, too:
$$-4 = x - 1 \quad\Rightarrow\quad -4 + 1 = x \quad\Rightarrow\quad x = -3$$

### Example 2: Multiplying and dividing
$$
3y = 18 \;\Rightarrow\; \frac{3y}{3} = \frac{18}{3} \;\Rightarrow\; y = 6
\qquad\qquad
\frac{n}{3} = -4 \;\Rightarrow\; 3 \times \frac{n}{3} = 3 \times (-4) \;\Rightarrow\; n = -12
$$

A sneaky one: $-v = 9$ means $-1v = 9$. Divide both sides by $-1$ to get $v = -9$.

## Two-step equations
When there are two things to undo, **undo the adding/subtracting first**, then the
multiplying/dividing. (BEDMAS in reverse.)

### Example 3: Solve and check $5x + 25 = 500$
$$
\begin{aligned}
5x + 25 &= 500 \\
5x + 25 - 25 &= 500 - 25 && \text{step 1: subtract 25 to isolate the } x\text{-term}\\
5x &= 475 \\
\frac{5x}{5} &= \frac{475}{5} && \text{step 2: divide by the coefficient, 5}\\
x &= 95
\end{aligned}
$$

**Check:** substitute $x = 95$ into each side separately.

| L.S. | R.S. |
|------|------|
| $5(95) + 25 = 475 + 25 = 500$ | $500$ |

L.S. = R.S., so $x = 95$ is correct ✓

### Example 4: Word problem
*The student council has 500 dollars to buy T-shirts at 6 dollars each. How many can they buy?*

Let $n$ = the number of T-shirts.
$$6n = 500 \;\Rightarrow\; n = \frac{500}{6} \approx 83.3$$
You can't buy part of a shirt, and 84 would cost too much, so **round down**:
**They can buy 83 T-shirts.**

## Watch out
- ⚠️ Check the sign of the coefficient: $-2x = 10$ gives $x = -5$, not $5$.
- ⚠️ In word problems, think about whether to round **up** or **down**. (Buses needed → round up. Items you can afford → round down.)

## Quick check
Solve and check.
1. $x - 7 = 12$
2. $\frac{h}{5} = -6$
3. $6c = -42$
4. $7x - 4 = 10$
5. $12g + 33 = 0$
6. Pies cost 7 dollars each. A customer spends 84 dollars. Write and solve an equation for the number of pies.

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. $x = 12 + 7 = 19$
2. $h = -6 \times 5 = -30$
3. $c = -42 \div 6 = -7$
4. $7x = 14$, so $x = 2$. Check: $7(2) - 4 = 10$ ✓
5. $12g = -33$, so $g = -\frac{33}{12} = -\frac{11}{4}$
6. $7p = 84$, so $p = 12$. The customer bought **12 pies**.

</details>
