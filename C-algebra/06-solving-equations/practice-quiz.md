# Practice Quiz: Solving Equations

> [⬅ Solving Equations](README.md) · Covers textbook Ch. 4 (§4.1–4.5)

Try every question on paper **before** opening the solution. Check each answer by substituting it back in.

## Part A: Simple equations (Lesson 1)

**1.** $x + 9 = 4$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$x = 4 - 9 = -5$

</details>

**2.** $-3y = 27$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$y = \frac{27}{-3} = -9$

</details>

**3.** $\frac{k}{4} = -3$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$k = -3 \times 4 = -12$

</details>

**4.** $4x - 7 = 21$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$4x = 28$, so $x = 7$. Check: $4(7) - 7 = 21$ ✓

</details>

**5.** $8 - 3n = 23$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$-3n = 15$, so $n = -5$. Check: $8 - 3(-5) = 8 + 15 = 23$ ✓

</details>

## Part B: Multi-step equations (Lesson 2)

**6.** $6a + 5 = 2a - 11$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$6a - 2a = -11 - 5 \Rightarrow 4a = -16 \Rightarrow a = -4$

</details>

**7.** $3(2x - 1) = 4x + 9$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$6x - 3 = 4x + 9 \Rightarrow 2x = 12 \Rightarrow x = 6$

</details>

**8.** $2(y + 4) - (y - 3) = 15$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$2y + 8 - y + 3 = 15 \Rightarrow y + 11 = 15 \Rightarrow y = 4$

</details>

**9.** $5 - 2(m - 1) = 3m - 8$ (solve and check)

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$
\begin{aligned}
5 - 2m + 2 &= 3m - 8 \\
7 - 2m &= 3m - 8 \\
15 &= 5m \\
m &= 3
\end{aligned}
$$
Check: L.S. $= 5 - 2(3 - 1) = 5 - 4 = 1$. R.S. $= 3(3) - 8 = 1$ ✓

</details>

## Part C: Fractions (Lesson 3)

**10.** $\frac{x}{3} + 2 = 6$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

Multiply every term by 3: $x + 6 = 18$, so $x = 12$.

</details>

**11.** $\frac{x + 1}{2} = \frac{x - 3}{4}$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

LCD = 4: $2(x + 1) = x - 3 \Rightarrow 2x + 2 = x - 3 \Rightarrow x = -5$.
Check: $\frac{-4}{2} = -2$ and $\frac{-8}{4} = -2$ ✓

</details>

**12.** $\frac{2}{3}(x - 6) = \frac{1}{2}(x + 1)$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

LCD = 6: $4(x - 6) = 3(x + 1) \Rightarrow 4x - 24 = 3x + 3 \Rightarrow x = 27$.
Check: $\frac{2}{3}(21) = 14$ and $\frac{1}{2}(28) = 14$ ✓

</details>

**13.** $\frac{x}{2} - \frac{x}{5} = 6$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

LCD = 10: $5x - 2x = 60 \Rightarrow 3x = 60 \Rightarrow x = 20$

</details>

## Part D: Formulas (Lesson 4)

**14.** Solve $A = \frac{1}{2}bh$ for $h$.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

Multiply by 2: $2A = bh$. Divide by $b$: $h = \dfrac{2A}{b}$

</details>

**15.** Solve $V = \pi r^2 h$ for $h$.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$h = \dfrac{V}{\pi r^2}$

</details>

**16.** Solve $y = mx + b$ for $m$.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$y - b = mx$, so $m = \dfrac{y - b}{x}$

</details>

## Part E: Word problems (Lesson 5)

**17.** Three consecutive even numbers add to 78. Find them.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$n + (n + 2) + (n + 4) = 78 \Rightarrow 3n + 6 = 78 \Rightarrow n = 24$. **24, 26 and 28.**

</details>

**18.** Phone plan A costs 25 dollars a month plus 10¢ per minute. Plan B costs 35¢ per minute with no monthly fee. For how many minutes do the plans cost the same?

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$$25 + 0.10m = 0.35m \;\Rightarrow\; 25 = 0.25m \;\Rightarrow\; m = 100$$
**They cost the same at 100 minutes** (35 dollars each).

</details>

**19.** In a triangle, one angle is 20° more than the smallest angle, and the third angle is twice the smallest. Find all three angles.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

$x + (x + 20) + 2x = 180 \Rightarrow 4x + 20 = 180 \Rightarrow x = 40$. **The angles are 40°, 60° and 80°.**

</details>

**20.** Is $x = 2$ a solution of $3(x + 1) = 5x - 1$? Show how you know.

<details><summary>Solution</summary>

L.S. $= 3(2 + 1) = 9$. R.S. $= 5(2) - 1 = 9$. L.S. = R.S., so **yes**.

</details>
