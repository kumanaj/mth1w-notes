# Algebraic Models With Tiles

> 📘 **Curriculum:** C1.2 · 📖 **Textbook:** §3.1, pp. 104–109 · [⬅ Strand C: Algebra](../)

## Big idea
**Algebra tiles** and **linking cubes** turn algebra into shapes: lengths ($x$),
areas ($x^2$) and volumes ($x^3$).

## Vocabulary
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **variable** | a quantity whose value can change (vary), usually represented by a letter |
| **algebraic expression** | a mathematical phrase made up of numbers and variables connected by addition or subtraction, e.g. $2x + 3$ |

## Key concepts
| Tile | Size | Represents |
|------|------|------------|
| unit tile (small square) | 1 × 1 | $+1$ (positive tiles are green); $-1$ (white) |
| $x$-tile (rectangle) | 1 × $x$ | $x$ or $-x$ |
| $x^2$-tile (big square) | $x$ × $x$ | $x^2$ or $-x^2$ |

- **Length models:** line up unit tiles and $x$-tiles. Skiing around a lake 3 times = $3x$ km.
- **Area models:** an $x^2$-tile has side length $x$, so its area is $x^2$. Mowing a square lawn 6 times = $6x^2$ m².
- **Volume models:** a cube with side $x$ has volume $x \cdot x \cdot x = x^3$. A cube of side 3 cm, built from linking cubes, has volume $3^3 = 27 \text{ cm}^3$.
- **Expression vs. equation:** $2x + 3$ is an *expression* (no equal sign); $2x + 3 = 1$ is an *equation*.

## Worked examples

### Example 1: Write the expression for a tile model
A model with 3 $x^2$-tiles, 5 $x$-tiles and 1 unit tile (all positive) → $3x^2 + 5x + 1$.
If the 2 unit tiles are white (negative) instead → $3x^2 + 5x - 2$.

### Example 2: Cube problems
*A cube has volume 216 cm³.*
- Side length: $s^3 = 216$, so $s = \sqrt[3]{216} = 6$ cm (since $6 \times 6 \times 6 = 216$).
- Area of one face: $6^2 = 36 \text{ cm}^2$.

*One face of a cube has area 49 m².*
- Side: $\sqrt{49} = 7$ m. Volume: $7^3 = 343 \text{ m}^3$.

## Watch out
- ⚠️ An $x$-tile and an $x^2$-tile are **different** things. You can't combine them into one kind.
- ⚠️ Squaring and taking the square root are **inverse operations**: $\sqrt{x^2} = x$ (for $x \ge 0$).

## Quick check
1. Write the expression: 2 $x^2$-tiles, 3 negative $x$-tiles, 4 positive unit tiles.
2. A cube has side length 4 cm. Write its volume as a power and evaluate it.
3. A cube has volume 8 cm³. What is its total surface area?

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. $2x^2 - 3x + 4$
2. $4^3 = 64 \text{ cm}^3$
3. Side $= 2$ cm, one face $= 4 \text{ cm}^2$, six faces $= 24 \text{ cm}^2$.

</details>
