# Terms and Polynomials

> 📘 **Curriculum:** C1.2 · 📖 **Textbook:** §3.4, pp. 130–139 · [⬅ Strand C: Algebra](../)

## Big idea
Learn the "parts of speech" of algebra (**terms, coefficients, polynomials, degree**) so you
can read, write and classify expressions precisely.

## Vocabulary
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **term** | an expression formed by the product of numbers and/or variables, e.g. $4x^2$ |
| **coefficient** | the number in a term (in $4x^2$ it's 4) |
| **variable part** | the letters and their exponents (in $4x^2$ it's $x^2$) |
| **polynomial** | an algebraic expression of one or more terms connected by addition or subtraction |
| **monomial / binomial / trinomial** | a polynomial with 1 / 2 / 3 terms |
| **degree of a term** | the **sum** of the exponents on the variables in the term |
| **degree of a polynomial** | the degree of its highest-degree term |

## Key concepts
$$\underset{\text{coefficient}}{4}\;\underset{\text{variable}}{x}^{\overset{\text{exponent}}{2}}$$

- A coefficient of 1 isn't written: $x^2$ means $1x^2$, and $-x$ means $-1x$.
- A constant like $5$ is a term of degree **0**.
- Classify polynomials **by number of terms**:

| Type | Number of terms | Examples |
|------|-----------------|----------|
| monomial | 1 | $3x$, $-5$, $2ab^2$ |
| binomial | 2 | $x + 4$, $3x^2 - 2y$ |
| trinomial | 3 | $x^2 + 3x - 1$ |
| polynomial | 4 or more | $x^3 + x^2 - x + 7$ |

(Memory aid: *mono* = 1, *bi* = 2, *tri* = 3, like a monorail, bicycle and tricycle.)

## Worked examples

### Example 1: Degree of a term
| Term | Exponents | Degree |
|------|-----------|--------|
| $x^2$ | 2 | 2 |
| $3y^4$ | 4 | 4 |
| $0.7u$ | 1 | 1 |
| $-2a^2b$ | $2 + 1$ | 3 |
| $\frac{2}{3}xy$ | $1 + 1$ | 2 |
| $5$ | no variable | 0 |

### Example 2: Degree of a polynomial
| Polynomial | Degrees of terms | Degree |
|------------|------------------|--------|
| $5x^2 - 2x$ | 2, 1 | **2** |
| $3y^3 + 0.2y - 1$ | 3, 1, 0 | **3** |
| $7x^2y^4 - x^6y$ | 6, 7 | **7** |

### Example 3: Write an expression
"Four times a number, decreased by 3" → $4n - 3$.
The perimeter of a rectangle with length $l$ and width $w$ → $2l + 2w$.

## Watch out
- ⚠️ In $x^2$ the coefficient is **1**, not 2. The 2 is the exponent.
- ⚠️ The degree of $7x^2y^4$ is $2 + 4 = 6$, not 4. **Add** the exponents of all the variables.
- ⚠️ $3x + 1$ and $3x$ are **not** equivalent, so the $+1$ must be written. A coefficient of 1 can be left out ($1t = t$), but a constant term of 1 can't.

## Quick check
1. Classify by number of terms and degree: $4x^3 - 2x + 9$
2. Classify: $-5ab^2$
3. What is the coefficient of $-y$?

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. Trinomial, degree 3.
2. Monomial, degree $1 + 2 = 3$.
3. $-1$

</details>
