# Collect Like Terms

> 📘 **Curriculum:** C1.3, C1.4 · 📖 **Textbook:** §3.5, pp. 144–153 · [⬅ Strand C: Algebra](../)

## Big idea
You can only add or subtract terms that are **alike**: the same variables with the same
exponents. Combine them by adding their coefficients.

## Vocabulary
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **like terms** | terms that have identical variables (and the same exponents), e.g. $3x^2$ and $-5x^2$ |
| **collecting like terms** | simplifying a polynomial by adding or subtracting like terms |

## Key concepts
- **Like:** $2x$ and $-3x$; $\;4ab$ and $ab$; $\;x^2$ and $7x^2$
- **Unlike:** $2x$ and $2x^2$ (different exponents); $\;3xy$ and $3x$ (different variables); $\;5$ and $5x$
- To collect like terms, add the **coefficients** and keep the **variable part unchanged**.
- Think "**add a negative**" when you see subtraction: $5x - 2x = 5x + (-2x) = 3x$.
- With algebra tiles, a positive and a negative tile of the same kind make a **zero pair**, so you remove both.
- Group like terms together (rearrange, keeping each term's sign) before combining.

## Worked examples

### Example 1: Identify like terms
In $2x,\; 2,\; 5x,\; 3xy,\; -3x$: the like terms are $2x, 5x, -3x$.

### Example 2: Add and subtract like terms
$$3x + 5x = 8x \qquad 4x^2 - x^2 = 3x^2 \qquad -2ab + 7ab = 5ab$$

### Example 3: Simplify a polynomial
$$
\begin{aligned}
&3x^2 + 4x - 2 - x^2 + 5 - 6x \\
&= (3x^2 - x^2) + (4x - 6x) + (-2 + 5) && \text{group like terms (keep signs)}\\
&= 2x^2 - 2x + 3
\end{aligned}
$$

### Example 4: Two variables
$$5a - 3b + 2a + b - 4 = 7a - 2b - 4$$

## Watch out
- ⚠️ $x + x = 2x$, **not** $x^2$. ($x \times x = x^2$.)
- ⚠️ $2x + 3x = 5x$, **not** $5x^2$. Exponents don't change when you add.
- ⚠️ $3ab + 2b \ne 5ab$. These are unlike terms, so leave them.
- ⚠️ $2x - 2x = 0$, not $x$ or $0x$.
- ⚠️ The sign in front of a term belongs to that term when you move it.

## Quick check
Simplify.
1. $4y - 7 + 2y + 3$
2. $x^2 + 3x - 5x^2 + x$
3. $2mn - 3m + 4mn + m - n$

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. $6y - 4$
2. $-4x^2 + 4x$
3. $6mn - 2m - n$

</details>
