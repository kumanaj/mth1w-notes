# Strand B: Number

> [⬅ Course home](../) · 📘 [Ontario MTH1W curriculum](https://www.dcp.edu.gov.on.ca/en/curriculum/secondary-mathematics/courses/mth1w)

How numbers are built and used: number sets, powers and exponents, and rational numbers (integers and fractions) with their applications to rates, percentages and proportions.

## Overall expectations

- **B1 Development of Numbers and Number Sets**: demonstrate an understanding of the development and use of numbers, and make connections between sets of numbers
- **B2 Powers**: represent numbers in various ways, evaluate powers, and simplify expressions by using the relationships between powers and their exponents
- **B3 Number Sense and Operations**: apply an understanding of rational numbers, ratios, rates, percentages, and proportions, in various mathematical contexts, and to solve problems

## Exponent cheat sheet
| Rule | Formula | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| Product rule | $x^a \times x^b = x^{a+b}$ | $3^2 \times 3^3 = 3^5$ |
| Quotient rule | $x^a \div x^b = x^{a-b}$ | $8^7 \div 8^5 = 8^2$ |
| Power of a power | $(x^a)^b = x^{ab}$ | $(3^2)^4 = 3^8$ |
| Zero exponent | $x^0 = 1$ ($x \ne 0$) | $\left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^0 = 1$ |
| Negative exponent | $x^{-n} = \frac{1}{x^n}$ | $2^{-3} = \frac{1}{8}$ |
| Scientific notation | $a \times 10^n$, $1 \le a < 10$ | $0.000\,342 = 3.42 \times 10^{-4}$ |

## Review checklist
- [ ] I can describe the number sets and how they're related.
- [ ] I can write powers in expanded form and evaluate them, including negative and fraction bases.
- [ ] I know why $(-3)^4 \ne -3^4$, and that $2^{-3}$ is positive.
- [ ] I can apply the exponent laws with numbers and variables.
- [ ] I can write very large and very small numbers in scientific notation.
- [ ] I can add, subtract, multiply and divide positive and negative fractions and mixed numbers.
- [ ] I can solve problems with rates, percentages and proportions.
